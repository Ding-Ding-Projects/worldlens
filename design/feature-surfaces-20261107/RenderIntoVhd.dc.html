How it runs

  1. Creates the VHDX, sized from the world, and mounts it to a private folder, no drive letter used.
  2. BlueMap writes tiles straight into it. If it fills, it grows and the render carries on.
  3. Detaches when the render ends, or keeps it attached for the viewer.
  4. The viewer, Publish and hosting attach it read-only when they need it.
